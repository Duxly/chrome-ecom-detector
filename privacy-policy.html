Privacy Policy

E-commerce Platform Detector by Duxly

Last Updated: April 3, 2026

TL;DR: This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All detection happens locally in your browser. Your privacy is 100% protected.

1. Introduction

Duxly ("we", "us", or "our") operates the E-commerce Platform Detector Chrome extension (the "Extension"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Extension.

2. Information We DO NOT Collect

The Extension is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your browsing history
  • URLs you visit
  • Personal information (name, email, etc.)
  • IP addresses
  • Cookies or tracking data
  • Any other personal or usage data

3. How the Extension Works

The Extension operates entirely locally in your browser:

  • Content Analysis: The Extension analyzes publicly visible HTML, JavaScript, and cookies on websites you visit to detect e-commerce platforms.
  • Local Processing: All detection happens in your browser. No data is sent to our servers or any third parties.
  • Temporary Storage: Detection results are stored temporarily in your browser's memory for the current tab only. They are cleared when you close the tab.
  • No External Communication: The Extension does not connect to any external servers or APIs.

4. Permissions Explained

The Extension requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • activeTab: Allows the Extension to analyze the current tab's content when you open the popup.
  • <all_urls> (host permissions): Required to detect platforms on any website you visit. This is necessary for the Extension to function but does not grant us access to your data.

These permissions are limited to what the Extension actually needs and do not enable data collection.

5. Third-Party Services

The Extension does not integrate with any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services. When you click the "Bekijk onze integraties" button in the popup, you are directed to our website (www.duxly.nl) in a new tab. Our website has its own privacy policy, separate from this Extension.

6. Data Security

Since we do not collect any data, there is no data to secure or protect. Your information never leaves your device.

7. Children's Privacy

The Extension does not knowingly collect information from children. Since we don't collect any data at all, it is safe for users of all ages.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the Extension and on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the Extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Open Source

The Extension's source code is available for review. You can verify our privacy claims by examining the code directly.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the Extension, please contact us:

  • Website: www.duxly.nl
  • Email: info@duxly.nl

11. Your Rights

Under GDPR and other privacy laws, you have rights regarding your personal data. However, since this Extension does not collect any personal data, these rights do not apply in this context. Your privacy is fully protected by design.
