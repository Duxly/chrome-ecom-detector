E-commerce Platform Detector by Duxly

Detect which e-commerce platform a website uses directly in your browser.

This extension checks public frontend signals on the page you are visiting and shows a simple badge when a supported platform is found. Click the extension icon to see the detected platform and available details.

WHAT IT DOES

• Detects e-commerce technology from publicly visible page signals
• Shows a clear badge on the extension icon when a platform is found
• Provides a simple popup with the detection result
• Works automatically while browsing
• Runs locally in your browser

HOW IT WORKS

The extension looks at frontend signals such as page structure, metadata, script sources, cookie names and browser-exposed variables. These checks happen locally. The extension does not need an external service to perform detection.

PRIVACY

The extension is designed to be lightweight and privacy-friendly:

• No account required
• No tracking
• No browsing history collection
• No external analytics
• No data sent to Duxly servers

WHO IT IS FOR

Useful for developers, agencies, e-commerce teams and anyone who wants a quick indication of the technology behind an online store.

INSTALLATION

Click “Add to Chrome” to install. No setup is required.
